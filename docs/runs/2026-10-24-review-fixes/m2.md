# Gesture and disposal regressions

Five new tests exercise the production Reader: long-press/drag across each of spreads 1|2 and 49|50, including retracing and gutter-only presses; just inside/outside the .08em ink-edge tolerance; short taps between two revealed words on one line; and completing a paused save after swiping from page 1 to page 7, then returning.

Spread assertions check swipe locking, both selected words' real semantics and black pixels, and count 1 for every saved word despite retracing. The blank tap toggles bars twice without re-greying either word. The pending-save test verifies grey before completion, actual old-page/notifier disposal (ChangeNotifier.debugAssertNotDisposed rejects it, proving disposal cleared listeners), no exception on completion, a new controller on return, and real revealed semantics/black pixels. No production change was needed. All five passed on the initial run (m2-first.log), and again after formatting/static typing (m2-green.log).
